## Recommended Action per Priority Group

**Critical**: Immediate, high-touch outreach (dedicated retention agent or account call). Offer a loyalty discount or contract upgrade incentive - these are the customers who cost the most to lose.

**High**: Proactive outreach via email/call campaign with a targeted offer (e.g. contract upgrade discount, free add-on trial). Automate where possible to manage cost.

**Low-Cost Retention**: Low-cost, automated retention: email nudges, self-serve loyalty discounts, or migration to autopay. Not worth expensive white-glove outreach given their spend level.

**Maintain**: No urgent action - maintain service quality and continue standard engagement (newsletters, loyalty perks) to protect this already-stable, valuable relationship.

**Monitor**: No immediate action, but include in periodic risk re-scoring - keep an eye out for any drop in engagement or a support complaint that could push them into a higher-risk tier.

**No Action Needed**: Minimal investment - standard service only. Revisit if their spend or risk profile changes.
